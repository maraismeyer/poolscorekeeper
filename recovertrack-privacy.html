RecoverTrack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026
The short version: RecoverTrack stores your information on your own device and in your personal iCloud account. We do not run servers that collect your data, we cannot see your information, and we never sell it or share it with third parties.

This Privacy Policy explains how RecoverTrack ("the app," "we," "us") handles your information. RecoverTrack is a personal wellness and recovery-tracking tool. Please read this alongside the in-app disclaimer, which explains that RecoverTrack is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

Information the app stores

RecoverTrack lets you record information related to your recovery, which may include:

  • Daily check-ins (pain, stiffness, sleep, energy, mood) and notes you enter
  • Body-map markings indicating where you experience pain or stiffness
  • Exercises, exercise programs, and logged activity
  • Medications, dosages, schedules, and doses you record
  • Appointments and medical test or imaging records, including any files or photos you choose to attach
  • Profile details you enter, such as your name, injury description, and provider information
  • Health and activity metrics read from Apple Health, if you grant permission (see below)

Where your information is stored

All of the information you enter is stored locally on your device and, if you use iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud account using Apple's iCloud/CloudKit. This means:

  • Your data stays under your control, on your devices and in your Apple account.
  • We do not operate any servers that receive, store, or process your information.
  • We have no ability to access, view, or retrieve your data.
  • Your iCloud data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you choose to connect Apple Health, RecoverTrack reads metrics such as workouts, steps, heart rate, sleep, and mobility measurements to show them alongside your recovery data. This access is optional and only happens after you grant permission.

  • Health data read from Apple Health is used only to display and summarize your progress within the app.
  • It is stored the same way as your other app data — on your device and your own iCloud.
  • We do not transmit your Health data to us or to any third party.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold.

Sharing reports

RecoverTrack can generate a report (for example, to share with your therapist or doctor). When you share a report, you choose where it goes using your device's standard share options. The app does not upload or send reports on your behalf.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect analytics or tracking data about you.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or marketing services.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles on any server.

Children's privacy

RecoverTrack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Because all data stays on the user's own device and iCloud, any information entered remains under the control of the device owner.

Your control over your data

  • You can view, edit, or delete the information you entered directly in the app.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your device; data synced to iCloud can be managed or removed through your iCloud settings.
  • You can disconnect Apple Health access at any time in the Settings app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, note the change within the app.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach us through the RecoverTrack support page.

Not medical advice. RecoverTrack is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or provide medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your treatment.
