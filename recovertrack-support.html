About

RecoverTrack helps you track your recovery — daily check-ins, pain and mobility, exercises, medications, and appointments — and share a clear report with your therapist or doctor. All of your data stays private on your device and your own iCloud.

Privacy

RecoverTrack stores all data locally on your device and in your personal iCloud account. We do not collect, share, or sell any personal information, and no account is required. Read the full Privacy Policy.
